# Impressions never leave the device

A Taster's Ratings, Descriptors and Notes are held only in their own browser and
are never sent anywhere. The product the Taster is here for is the Carousel, and
the group explicitly does not want the review data, so persisting Impressions
would buy nothing while dragging in a database of personal opinions, an LGPD
consent story, and a privacy policy.

## Consequences

The group can never answer "which bottle did people like most last night?" —
that is accepted, and group-facing analytics are out of scope for this effort.
Adding server-side Impressions later is additive (one POST), not a redesign.

**Do not "fix" the missing reviews table.** Its absence is the decision.
